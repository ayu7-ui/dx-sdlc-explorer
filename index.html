01 / Time allocation

The work that moves the business forward competes with the work that keeps it running.

Each industry view shows how a developer’s year is allocated across innovation, keeping the lights on, and other work from Q1 through Q4 FY26.

02 / Deep focus

The scarcest resource is a block of time no one interrupts.

Focus is counted as a continuous 90-minute or longer period. See both the space available for demanding work and the recurring friction that breaks it apart.

03 / Driver impact

Not every facet of the developer experience has the same effect.

Plot perceived strength against its relative impact to identify where improving the developer experience is most likely to improve outcomes.

04 / Read the data well

Use the explorer to see the pattern. Do not mistake it for causation.

This concept uses synthetic benchmark data for design testing. In a production view, every finding should show its sample, time range, source, and metric definition. Activity signals describe work; they do not, on their own, prove business value.
